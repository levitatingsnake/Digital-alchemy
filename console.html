05 / EXPERIMENT LEDGER

Predictions meet reality

Record what you expected before the test, then what actually happened. This creates an auditable feedback loop instead of silently rewriting old assumptions.
Local-first
A recommendation was carried over from Decision Lab. Review the proposed hypothesis and choose a meaningful measure before recording anything.
Numeric error is calculated as actual minus predicted. Percentage error uses the absolute difference divided by the absolute prediction. If the prediction is zero, percentage error is shown as unavailable. These are descriptive comparisons, not causal proof. The records stay in this browser unless exported.
